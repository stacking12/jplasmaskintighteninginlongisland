About Our J-Plasma Skin Tightening

J-Plasma is a precise, minimally invasive technology that uses cold helium plasma energy to heat the deeper layers of skin and trigger new collagen production.

At Millennial Plastic Surgery in Great Neck, we use this approach, also known as Renuvion skin tightening, to treat mild to moderate skin laxity. It is a good fit for patients who want a visible improvement but are not ready for a full surgical lift.

Every treatment starts with a one-on-one consultation. We look at your skin, your goals and your health history, then recommend a plan that fits you. Results develop gradually as your body builds new collagen over the following months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about J-Plasma skin tightening. Your own consultation will cover the details for your situation.

What is J-Plasma skin tightening?

J-Plasma skin tightening uses cold helium plasma energy to heat the deeper layers of skin. The heat causes the skin to contract and signals the body to produce new collagen, which improves firmness over time.

Is J-Plasma the same as Renuvion?

They are closely related. Renuvion is the brand name of the technology that combines helium plasma with radiofrequency energy. Many people use the terms interchangeably when they search for skin tightening options.

Who is a good candidate?

Patients with mild to moderate skin laxity on the face, neck or body often do well. People who have a large amount of excess skin may need a surgical option instead. A consultation is the best way to find out which fits you.

What is recovery like?

Recovery varies by treatment area and the extent of the procedure. Some swelling, redness or tenderness is common at first. We give you personalized aftercare instructions and explain what to expect before you book.

When will I see results?

Some tightening can be noticed early, but the full effect develops gradually over several months as new collagen builds. Individual results vary, and we cannot guarantee a specific outcome.
